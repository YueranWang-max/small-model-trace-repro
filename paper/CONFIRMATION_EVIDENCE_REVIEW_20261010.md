# Confirmation evidence review and manuscript inclusion plan

## Verified primary benefit

The frozen independent confirmation criterion was met: prefix versus complete reversible compact-direct input increased graph-mean F1 by 0.1161334308 (descriptive stratified graph-bootstrap95% interval0.0437471685 to0.1913687766; two-sided paired sign-flip p0.0062399376). Twenty-four graph units are independent sampling units; the two seeds within each graph are repeated measurements. The comparison is a package intervention: trace content and its warning wrapper are added along with preparation. It is not evidence for a pure placement or latent-state mechanism.

## Costs and stronger baselines

Complete source preparation is charged to every reused answer. Prefix uses1.3978835633 times compact-direct model tokens (interval1.1696928012 to1.6670597363); the primary accuracy benefit costs additional tokens. The paired wall-time interval crosses1, so do not claim faster execution than compact-direct. Prefix has higher F1 and fewer complete model tokens than both original-direct baselines, including contemporaneous16384-cap original-long. Those contrasts were prespecified secondary comparisons, unadjusted for multiplicity. Their results cannot be relabeled primary or described as equal-compute superiority, money savings or broad deployment gains. Direct compact recoding also improves the original baseline; do not credit all combined improvement to reuse.
