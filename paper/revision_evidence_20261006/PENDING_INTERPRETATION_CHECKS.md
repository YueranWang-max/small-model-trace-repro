# Checks to apply after the frozen follow-ups terminate

- The same twelve graph questions are reused across models: there are twelve question units, not twenty-four independent graphs. Seeds are repeated measurements, not extra graph units.
- Each package generates its own five traces. The matched local configurations compare pipelines; they do not isolate reader size, parameter count, pretraining or architecture.
- Prefix versus append holds complete graph, actual trace content and warning wrapper fixed. Prefix versus direct adds content, warning instructions and preparation compute, so it is a secondary pipeline contrast.
- Source scores require a normally stopped visible answer. A zero source score caused by output termination is not proof that its thinking contains no useful evidence. Native thinking and visible answers remain distinct.
- Report normal-valid counts, exact match, F1, truncations and always-empty baseline separately. No score-dependent stopping, post hoc parser replacement or answer extraction from thinking.
- A placement contrast at a score ceiling does not establish population equivalence. A collapsed descriptive bootstrap interval on a tiny fixed sample cannot supply broader evidence than that sample.
- The context diagnostic deliberately reuses the known row-506 failure. A successful full-input probe establishes declared input accounting/residency under 196608 context, not quality, and does not complete the old sixteen-graph trial.
- All source preparation is charged for one deployment answer. Physical new-run usage, previously incurred source cost and request-wall timing must be disclosed separately. No energy or exclusive-hardware claim.
- Local hashes and model metadata establish consistency, not third-party execution attestation. Missing historical outputs stay missing and are excluded from new empirical claims.
- Final text follows the original paper's question/intervention/matched-results/boundary structure. No copied language or claim that a replication must invent a new method.

- The output-budget follow-up was selected after interim truncation, uses the same known questions and runs sequentially. Its 28672 cap matches maximum generated-token allowances only. Report it as a diagnostic rather than fresh confirmation or equal-total-compute benchmarking. Preserve the original 8192-cap comparator and all sixty long-cap planned outcomes.
