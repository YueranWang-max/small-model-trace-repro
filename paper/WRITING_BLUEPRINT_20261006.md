# Writing blueprint and limits

The structural reference is Zou and Tang, *Trace as State*, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02702v1. Its useful pattern is a concrete ordering question, defined same-trace placement intervention, matched quantitative observations, control experiments, then bounded interpretation. The present report adopts that progression, not the original authors' sentences or theoretical innovation.

The user-supplied writing article is treated as practical guidance, not evidence that all journals require a fixed formula:

- Introduction: four focused paragraphs move from reasoning reuse to the local-model gap, retained/adapted intervention and the replication question.
- Abstract: names the task, local models and completed allocation, reports F1/EM and uncertainty, then states what the bounded observations support. Planned work must not appear as a result.
- Methods: actual source, model package/runtime, complete prompt/trace controls, sampling and output/context limits, parser, missingness, unit of analysis and resource accounting.
- Results: completed matched studies lead. F1, exact match, normal-valid format and termination are separate. Diagnostic tables preserve all frozen conditions. Historical interrupted detail remains visible but mainly in appendices.
- Discussion: connects placement evidence to the original method, distinguishes source competence, reader execution and scoring, and states limitations without interpreting uncertainty as proof of no effect.
- Conclusion: short, introduces no new data or citations, and restricts the claim to the tested configurations.

The central value is a reproducible local boundary study. It need not demonstrate a new method, a universal improvement, long-document comprehension, or lower equal-compute cost. Final review incorporated both completed diagnostics, narrowed the mechanism wording and rewrote the opening around a plain placement question. The larger-output direct result is included in the abstract and conclusion. Teacher feedback is mapped in TEACHER_AND_WRITING_CHECKLIST_20261006.md. Simulated reviews are not external peer review.
