# Pre-submission simulated review: round 1

## Review setup
Input: current 18-page manuscript, archived evidence and completed prospective 1.7B study. The paired 4B and capacity diagnostic are running/planned, not findings. Three assessments are simulated emphases, not independent people or editorial decisions. Nature-style axes are used for rigor/readability; Nature-level significance is not a prerequisite asserted for arXiv. Main claim: local replication conditions and observed boundaries, without universal improvement.

## Reviewer 1 — technical soundness emphasis
Overall: qualified but narrow evidence. Interested readers: researchers reproducing reasoning-feedback methods on constrained local models.
Strengths: identical T placement controls, full retained source, graph-level inference, captured failures and independent scoring.
Major concerns: only one prospective model completed; same short selected population; older interrupted branches must not be described as repaired. The context-limit claim requires distinguishing configured overflow from inability to run with a larger allocation. Author identity remains unverified.
Technical failings to address: complete planned second-model comparison, diagnose the retained overflow case under an explicit changed allocation, audit request/response mappings and release dependencies. No missing answer may be reconstructed as the old observation.
Axes: incremental originality; field-local importance; limited interdisciplinary reach; mostly sound with unresolved scope; readable but archive-heavy.
Recommendation posture: revise; no acceptance prediction.

## Reviewer 2 — originality and importance emphasis
Overall: valuable as a bounded reproducibility report, not established method innovation. Interested readers: inference practitioners deciding whether five traces justify local resources.
Strengths: credit to original method; explicit preparation cost and exact-match/F1 distinction.
Major concerns: uncertainty does not establish absence of effect; short graph performance is not long-document comprehension. Do not run until significance or use exploratory route gates as a definition of reproducibility.
Technical failings to address: maintain fixed sample sizes and the graph unit; distinguish within-model comparisons from a pure size effect; document empty-answer baseline and truncated sources.
Axes: new local observations rather than a new reasoning principle; limited significance beyond local deployment; uncertain broad interest; evidence interpretable under stated bounds; presentation should lead with completed studies.
Recommendation posture: a focused archival report may be defensible after revision; no settled Nature fit.

## Reviewer 3 — readership and readability emphasis
Overall: understandable central question but too many historical branches interrupt the narrative. Interested readers: local-model users and replication researchers.
Strengths: clear placement notation, numeric abstract and short conclusion.
Major concerns: gate catalogues and partial-state histories compete with completed comparisons; unsigned local hashes are consistency evidence, not execution attestation. Placeholder authors prevent an actual submission.
Technical failings to address: move supporting incomplete branches to an appendix, summarize the core evidence in a compact table, retain separate accuracy/feasibility claims. Verify figure readability and all bundled dependencies.
Axes: modest originality, local importance, narrow readership, transparent but incomplete technical coverage, improvement possible through compression.
Recommendation posture: revise to focus; no editorial decision implied.

## Cross-review synthesis
Consensus strengths: matched placement controls, intact graph, trace/output provenance and honest uncertainty.
Consensus risks: scope/model coverage, conflating configured overflow with absolute infeasibility, historical distractions, unverified personal metadata.
Emphasis differs only in the weighting of rigor, significance and readability.
Priority work: complete the finite paired-model and capacity diagnostics; independent audits; focused writing and arXiv technical verification. The work need not discover a novel method to meet its stated replication objective, and a null/uncertain result must remain publishable evidence rather than prompt open-ended score chasing.

## Risk / unsupported claims
Universal small-model advantage, architecture-only mechanisms, lossless document compression, historical response recovery, guaranteed arXiv acceptance and confirmed authorship are unsupported. No proposed experiment is represented as completed.
